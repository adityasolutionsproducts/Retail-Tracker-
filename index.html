Shop Information
Appearance

When on, the app automatically switches between light and dark to match your phone/browser setting. You can still tap the moon/sun icon at the top to override it anytime.

Theme

Pick a color theme — every option keeps the same frosted-glass look, just with different colors. Works with both light and dark mode.

Teal Classic
Ocean Blue
Royal Purple
Sunset Coral
Emerald
Rose Gold
Custom
Custom Brand Color

Pick your shop's brand colors — buttons, highlights, and charts across the app will use them. Changing a color here automatically switches you to the "Custom" theme above.

Discounts

When off, product discounts won't be applied during billing, on the stock list, or on newly printed stickers — even if a discount % is set on a product.

Tax / GST

Tax is calculated on (Subtotal − Discount) and added to the invoice total. You can override the rate per invoice at billing time.

Payment QR

Let customers scan a QR code to pay you directly at checkout, instead of typing a number by hand.

A static QR (like a bKash "Send Money" QR) can't encode the amount automatically — the customer will need to type the amount themselves after scanning. A UPI ID QR fills the amount in for them.

Thermal Printer

Print receipts directly to a Bluetooth ESC/POS thermal printer (58mm/80mm) instead of downloading a PDF each time.

Not connected

Only works in Chrome/Edge on Android or Desktop over HTTPS — Safari and iOS don't support Web Bluetooth, so this option won't appear there. Also note: most budget thermal printers only print Latin characters properly — Bengali item/customer names may come out blank or garbled depending on your printer's font support.

Loyalty Points / Rewards

Customers earn points automatically on new sales (based on invoice total) and can redeem them for a discount at billing.

Due Reminder Message

Placeholders: {name}, {due}, {shop}. Country code is used to format numbers for WhatsApp links if customers' saved numbers don't include one.

Locations / Warehouses

Manage multiple shops/warehouses. Select a location while billing or receiving purchases to track stock per location, and use Stock Transfer to move goods between them.

Notifications & Automation

Alerts and the daily summary work while this app is open in your browser (as a tab or installed PWA) — there's no separate server, so they can't wake your phone when the app/tab is fully closed, the way a bank app's push notification might.

Checking permission…

Backup

Download all your data (products, sales, customers, expenses) as a single Excel file.

No backup recorded yet.

Since there's no background server, this only triggers when you open the app in your browser after the scheduled interval has passed — it will auto-download a fresh backup file for you at that point, rather than truly running while the app is closed.

Restore from Backup

Upload a previously downloaded backup .xlsx file. Records are matched by their original ID — matching records get overwritten with the backup's data, and anything not in the file is left untouched (nothing already in your account gets deleted).
